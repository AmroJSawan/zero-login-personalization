Zero-Login Signal Surface

Everything this page can determine about you with no account, no login, and no permission prompt. Nothing here is transmitted anywhere: all of it is computed locally in your browser. The tables are the first-shot surface, available before you do anything. The right-hand panel is the temporal layer: move your mouse, scroll, and type in the box, and watch the session climb the knowledge ladder from T0 toward T5, the point at which published results say frustration becomes reliably classifiable.
